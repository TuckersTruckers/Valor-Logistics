ABOUT VALOR

Freight service built on trust.

Valor Logistics is a veteran and family-owned freight brokerage focused on dependable truckload transportation and straightforward communication.

A personal approach to logistics

We are a locally owned team that takes the time to understand each shipment, match it with the right available carrier, and keep everyone informed from pickup through delivery. Our size allows us to stay responsive and accountable without overcomplicating the process.

We primarily arrange full truckload dry van, reefer, and flatbed freight. When your shipment calls for another common truck type—such as a box truck or hot shot—we can help identify and book an appropriate option when available.

Dependable communication

Clear updates, practical expectations, and a team that stays involved when plans change.

Right-sized solutions

We focus on the transportation needs we know well instead of promising services outside our capabilities.

People you can reach

Work directly with an experienced local team committed to earning repeat business.

OUR COMMITMENT

Professional service, without the corporate runaround.

Whether you ship every day or need help with one load, our goal is the same: make transportation easier, keep communication moving, and deliver a reliable experience.
